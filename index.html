HOW TO PLAY

The dead come in rounds, and every round there are more of them and they're harder to kill. Shoot them to earn points. Spend points on weapons chalked on the walls, on clearing debris to open new areas, on perk machines, and on the Mystery Box, which gives you a random weapon. If you're lucky, that's the rare Ray Gun.

Zombies break through the boarded windows. Stand at a window and hold F to nail the boards back up for a few extra points. Headshots and knife kills pay more. Glowing green power-ups sometimes drop from the dead.
